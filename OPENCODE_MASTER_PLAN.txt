================================================================================
You must write CSS/HTML that strictly adheres to these modern UI guidelines:
1. OVERALL AESTHETIC: Premium SaaS productivity + playful intelligence. Clean, fast, and restrained.
2. SURFACES & CONTAINERS: 
   - ZERO 3D effects. ZERO heavy drop shadows. NO neumorphism.
   - Use ultra-thin `1px solid` borders to separate cards (`#E5E7EB` in light mode, `#334155` in dark mode).
   - Avoid "card-inside-card" clutter.
3. SHAPES & CORNERS: 
   - Minimalist squares with slight softening. 
   - Use `border-radius: 4px` or `6px` max for buttons and cards. NO fully rounded/pill-shaped "fatty" buttons.

   - Dark Mode: Backgrounds `#0F172A` (Slate 900) or `#1E293B`. Text `#F8FAFC`.
   - Light Mode: Backgrounds `#F8FAFC` or `#FFFFFF`. Text `#0F172A`.

   - Muted/Secondary: Slate Grays (`#64748B`).
5. TYPOGRAPHY:
   - Font Family: Inter, Geist, or system-ui.
   - Hierarchy: High contrast bold headers (`font-weight: 700`), crisp regular body text (`font-weight: 400`), muted secondary labels.
6. ANIMATIONS & INTERACTIONS:
   - ONLY 200ms-300ms `ease-in-out` transitions for opacity, background-color, and border-color.
   - NO bouncing, sliding, or heavy keyframe animations.
7. LOADING STATES:
   - Use Skeleton Loaders (flat pulsing blocks with `#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1` in light mode). NO spinning circles.
