Quick answers

Do I need to be right? No. Challenges are social prompts, not exams.

Can I change my answer? Yes. Your saved choice can be changed and the community result updates.

Where do comments go? Challenge discussion is visible to signed-in users; Private Chat stays one-to-one.

Can I delete my comment? You can delete your own Challenge discussion comment.

How do I create one? Open Create, choose a format, add a twist, preview it and publish.

🔐

Login & account

Email/password, Google and Trio UID login are supported. Repeated login attempts can be temporarily limited for account safety. Firebase also applies its own abuse protections.

If you forget your email password, use Forgot password? on the login page. If Trio UID login is temporarily limited, wait for the cooldown instead of repeatedly retrying.

🛡

Privacy & safety

Keep passwords, OTPs, financial credentials and private addresses out of Stories and Chat. Report or stop interacting with content that makes you uncomfortable.

Privacy Policy · Terms & Conditions

Temporary Chat

Opposite-pick Challenge Chat is temporary and can expire after 10 minutes. An expired room cannot be used even before cleanup finishes.

🧹

Stories & expiry

Some ephemeral Story/Voice items carry server-side expiry metadata. Cleanup runs separately from the UI, so an expired item may disappear from storage shortly after its expiry time.
