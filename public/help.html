Quick answers

Do I need to create a room?

No. Every activity can be started solo. Use a room when you want other people to solve, build, learn or compete with you.

What happens when an activity expires?

It leaves active discovery. The activity cycle does not stay in the active feed forever, which keeps Forge fresh.

What happens to a live room?

Rooms are short-lived. An inactive or expired room should disappear from the live list rather than becoming permanent clutter.

How do I report something?

Use the Help Center and the relevant moderation/report controls inside the app where available. Do not post personal information in public activity descriptions or room chat.
