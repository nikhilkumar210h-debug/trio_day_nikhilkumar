Quick answers

Do I need to be right? No. Challenges are social prompts, not exams.

Can I change my answer? Yes. Your saved choice can be changed and the community result updates.

Where do comments go? Challenge discussion is public to signed-in users; private Chat stays one-to-one.

Can I delete my comment? You can delete your own Challenge discussion comment.

How do I create one? Open Create and use Moment Builder to choose a format, add a twist, preview it, and publish.
