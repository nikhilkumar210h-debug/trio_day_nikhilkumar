Quick Answers

Do I need to login? No — guests get 3 free tasks. Login to save XP, pick a growth path, and unlock all activities.

What counts as "proof"? A short reflection, a link to your work, or a description of what you did. AI verifies it matches the task.

Can I skip a task? Yes — Skip gives 0 XP and moves to the next task.

How does the leaderboard work? Weekly XP (resets Sunday). Top 3 get floating podium cards; full list shows everyone.

Where is my data stored? Activities & tasks in Supabase. Media in Cloudinary. User accounts & XP in Firebase.

🔐

Login & Account

Email/password and Google sign-in supported. Firebase handles auth; repeated attempts are rate-limited for safety.

Forgot password? Use Forgot password? on the login page.

Privacy & Safety

Keep passwords, OTPs, financial credentials and private addresses out of task proofs and chat. Report content that makes you uncomfortable.
