PRIVACY POLICY

Your data. Clear rules.

Effective date: 29 September 2026. This policy explains what Trio Day collects, why it is used, where it is processed, and the controls available to users.

01

Account data

When you create or use an account, Trio Day may process your name, email address, Firebase user ID, permanent Trio UID, profile photo, bio, authentication provider and account timestamps.

Email is kept in the private user record; the public profile does not intentionally expose the account email.

02

Content you create

Challenges, answers, discussion comments, Stories, profile information and private messages are stored so the features you use can work.

Public Challenge discussions and public Stories can be visible to other signed-in users according to their configured visibility.

03

Media

Images and videos uploaded for supported features are stored with the configured media provider, currently Cloudinary. Story metadata is stored in Firebase Firestore.

Temporary or expiring content is designed to be removed by the app's server-side cleanup process after its expiry window.

04

Private Chat

Private Chat is one-to-one. Access is restricted by Firebase security rules and messages are stored under the participating accounts.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, financial information, exact private addresses or other sensitive information through Chat or Stories.

05

How we use data

Data is used to authenticate accounts, operate Challenges, show profiles and connections, deliver Chat and notifications, calculate valid progress, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures and maintain security.

Trio Day does not use your content to create targeted advertising profiles.

06

Service providers

Trio Day uses Firebase for authentication and database services, Cloudflare Workers for selected server-side operations and Cloudinary for configured media storage. Google sign-in is available as an authentication provider.

These providers process data as needed to provide their respective services.

07

Security & abuse prevention

Authentication and protected operations use Firebase security controls and server-side checks. Login attempts are subject to Firebase's built-in abuse protections and Trio Day's additional login controls.

Security-related records may be retained when necessary to protect accounts, investigate abuse or maintain service reliability.

08

Local storage & cookies

The web app may use browser storage for preferences, short-lived caches, theme selection and login-rate-limit state. These values help the app work smoothly and are not a substitute for account records.

09

Retention & deletion

We retain account and content data while it is needed to operate the service, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or preserve security. Expiring content is handled by its server-side expiry process.

Deleting an account or content may not instantly remove every backup, log or third-party copy.

10

Children & age requirements

Users must follow the age and consent requirements that apply where they live. Where applicable law treats a user as a child, required parent or lawful-guardian consent must be obtained before processing that child's personal data.

Trio Day does not knowingly request unnecessary sensitive information from children and does not use children's data for targeted advertising.

11

Your choices

You can control the profile, Story, Challenge and Chat information exposed by the current UI. You can change or delete content where the feature provides that control.

For account, access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, use the Help Center and the support channel made available by Trio Day.

12

Policy changes

This policy may change as Trio Day's features, providers or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be updated when the policy is materially revised.

Important

This policy describes the current Trio Day product and deployment configuration. It should be read together with the Terms & Conditions. If a mandatory local privacy rule gives you a stronger right than this policy describes, that mandatory rule controls.
