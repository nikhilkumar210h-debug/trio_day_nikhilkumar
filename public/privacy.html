DATA & PRIVACY

Your data. Clear rules.

Last updated: 19 September 2026. This page describes the current Trio Day app behavior and should be reviewed before public launch as the product changes.

01

What we store

Account: sign-in identity and basic profile fields such as name, profile photo, user ID and bio.

Content: posts, comments, reactions, activity descriptions and community-created activities.

Activity use: completions, room membership, ratings and progress needed to run the product.

Rooms: room name, activity, participant list and room chat messages while the room exists.

02

Media

When media upload is enabled, image/media files are sent to the configured media provider used by Trio Day, currently Cloudinary in the app architecture. Firestore stores small metadata and references rather than acting as the media file store.

Do not upload private documents, passwords, payment details or other sensitive material to public posts, activities or room chat.

03

How information is shared

Some information is intentionally social. Public posts and public activity information can be visible to other signed-in users. Room members can see activity and room information needed for the session, including chat.

Trio Day may rely on service providers such as Firebase and Cloudinary to authenticate users, store app data, deliver content and operate the service.

04

Retention

Live activities and rooms are designed around finite cycles so the active feed does not grow forever. Expired items can leave active discovery while historical records may remain where required for account history, moderation or product functionality.

Chat and media retention may change as the app evolves. The current product should not be treated as an archival service.

05

Your controls

You can manage the profile/content controls exposed by the app, stop participating in rooms, and remove content where the current UI provides a delete control.

For account or privacy questions, start from the Help Center so the request can be routed with the product’s current controls.

06

Safety

Do not share passwords, one-time codes, financial information, private addresses or other highly sensitive personal information in posts, activities or room chat.

If you see content that should not be on the platform, use the available moderation/report controls and avoid redistributing it.

Important

This is the current product-policy draft for the MVP. Before a public launch, the owner should align it with the exact Firebase, Cloudinary, authentication, retention and moderation configuration, plus applicable privacy laws and age requirements.
